Graduate Students

I am looking to admit at least two graduate students for Fall 2027 (and potentially more) in one of several areas:

  1. Evaluation of AI
  2. Human-AI teaming
  3. Efficient approaches to language modeling / QA
  4. Multimodal QA (both methods and datasets)

The ideal candidate is someone who is very technically involved in the workings of modern NLP methods and is interested in applying them to human-centered tasks like human--computer cooperation for question answering or measuring interpretability. More broadly, I'm looking for technically strong people who are also interested in the beauty of language but not afraid of the messy ways language is applied in the real world. Experience with, or fondness for, recreational question answering (trivia, puzzles, language games, etc.) is a plus.
