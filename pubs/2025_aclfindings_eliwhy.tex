    {\bf Accessible Abstract:} We investigated the ability of language models to tailor responses for learners with varied informational needs and knowledge backgrounds. This research introduced ELI-Why, a benchmark of 13.4K "Why" questions to evaluate the pedagogical capabilities of language models. We conducted two extensive human studies to assess the utility of language model-generated explanatory answers (explanations) on our benchmark, tailored to three distinct educational grades: elementary, high-school and graduate school. We find that GPT-4-generated explanations match their intended educational background about half the time. Across all educational backgrounds, users deemed GPT-4-generated explanations less suited on average to their informational needs, when compared to explanations curated by lay people.\\~
